About NCPOR Outreach

The National Centre for Polar and Ocean Research (NCPOR) is committed to sharing the wonders of polar and ocean science with society through diverse outreach programs.

🧑‍🏫

Workshops

Hands-on learning experiences for students and educators on polar and ocean topics.

🏛️

Exhibitions

Interactive displays showcasing cutting-edge polar and ocean research discoveries.

🏃

Field Visits

Immersive experiences connecting participants with real-world research environments.

🎤

Scientific Talks

Expert lectures and discussions on polar science, climate change, and ocean conservation.
