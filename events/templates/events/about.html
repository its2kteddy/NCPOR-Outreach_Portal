About NCPOR Outreach

Bridging cutting-edge polar and ocean research with society through engaging outreach programs.

Our Mission

The National Centre for Polar and Ocean Research (NCPOR) is India's premier institution for polar and Southern Ocean research. Our outreach program is dedicated to sharing the excitement of scientific discovery with students, educators, and the general public.

Through interactive workshops, exhibitions, field visits, and expert talks, we aim to inspire the next generation of scientists and foster public understanding of polar and ocean science, climate change, and environmental conservation.

🌍

Engage students and educators in polar science

Promote awareness of climate change and ocean conservation

Foster scientific temper and curiosity

Build partnerships with educational institutions

Our Journey

2018

Launch of Outreach Program

NCPOR officially launched its dedicated outreach initiative to connect with schools and colleges.

2019

First National Workshop

Conducted the first large-scale workshop on polar science with 500+ student participants from across India.

2021

Virtual Outreach Initiative

Adapted to virtual platforms during the pandemic, reaching participants nationwide with online sessions.

2023

50+ Partner Institutions

Established collaborations with over 50 educational institutions across India for regular outreach activities.

2025

Outreach Portal Launch

Launched this comprehensive portal to showcase events, share media, and facilitate broader engagement.

Our Focus Areas

❄️

Polar Research

Sharing insights from India's expeditions to the Arctic and Antarctic, including research on ice dynamics, glaciology, and polar ecosystems. Students learn about life in extreme environments and the importance of polar regions in global climate systems.

🌊

Ocean Literacy

Promoting understanding of ocean processes, marine biodiversity, and the critical role oceans play in regulating Earth's climate. Programs cover topics from ocean currents to deep-sea exploration and sustainable fisheries.

🌡️

Climate Education

Educating communities about climate change, its impacts, and mitigation strategies. Interactive sessions explain how polar ice melt affects sea levels, and how ocean warming influences weather patterns and marine ecosystems worldwide.

🔬

Citizen Science

Encouraging public participation in scientific research through data collection, observation programs, and collaborative projects. Participants contribute to real research while developing scientific skills and environmental awareness.
